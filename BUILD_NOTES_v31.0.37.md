- Establishes one canonical Featured Competition turning point and supplies it to both the report presentation and Story of the Round.

- Preserves previously accepted and frozen Ledger Entries without regeneration or historical mutation.
- Removes meaningless Out, In, and Total sums from Appendix stroke-index rows while retaining every per-hole stroke index.
- Reduces the iPhone and iPad Safari print surface to the browser-safe printable height to prevent an extra physical sheet after each designed report page.
- Adds focused regression coverage for permanent-lead selection, tied matches, Story/report alignment, scorecard metadata, and print-page sizing.

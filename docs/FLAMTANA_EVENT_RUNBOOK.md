# Flamtana Special — Friday, October 16, 2026

**Install v31.0.73 on both phones before the event.** New matches use off-low
strokes; .72 adds AI Story/report and remote-completion fixes. Existing
unfinished matches retain their original full-handicap wager rule; start a fresh
match to use the new rule. Existing frozen results stay unchanged.
Keep a paper team scorecard and the eight Calcutta picks with the host.

## 1. Before anyone tees off

1. On both phones, open **More → App Support** and confirm v31.0.73. Install any
   available update before creating the event match. Confirm existing saved rounds
   are still in Library. Check **Technical diagnostics** for storage usage.
   Check **More → Account & Security**: the host must be signed into the account
   that creates the round. If Play/Scores asks you to sign in, use its Account &
   Security button, then return to the saved round and tap **Sync Now**. Do not
   recreate the round or clear app data. Other access-denied messages may need
   account/assignment checks; they do not necessarily mean you are signed out.
2. On the host, choose **Match → Create a Match**. Select the course and 18 holes.
   Select **Flamtana Special / Two-man Scramble**, four teams of two, a Shared
   Match and assigned-player scoring. Use only Flamtana Special for the wagers.
3. Select all eight distinct golfers and team names. Choose **one tee per team**;
   both partners share that tee, while different teams may choose different tees.
   Deliberately check/enter each assigned team index, resulting Course HCP and Gets.
   For a new match, Gets is that team's Course HCP minus the lowest of all four.
4. In **Games & Stat Tracking**, check the per-person Featured, Group, Foursome
   and Calcutta stakes and the tie method. Defaults are $20 each: $80 exposure per
   golfer. Enter **all eight Calcutta picks**, including anyone backing their own
   team. Write the stakes, tie method and picks on the paper card.
5. Create the shared round with **Start Round**. This creates the match; it is
   separate from **Start Scoring**. Give the displayed match code to the other phone.
6. On the other phone, choose **Match → Join a Match**, enter a clear device name
   and the code, then join. Confirm the actual course, eight golfers and team rows
   appear on that phone. Green on the host only means recently seen; it does not
   prove the other phone downloaded and saved the match.
7. On the host, open **Manage Assignments**. Assign **all four golfers in T1 + T2
   to the first device**, and **all four in T3 + T4 to the second device**. Assign
   both partners on every team; assigning one golfer per team is insufficient.
8. Check all eight assignments and all eight picks. Tap **Start Scoring** on the
   host. Confirm the other phone can edit T3/T4 and has the correct hole selected.
   Do a rehearsal before Friday; do not rely on the green indicator alone.

## 2. During play

- Enter **one gross shared-ball score per team**. The app writes both partners.
  An empty score's first + or − tap brings up par; adjust from there. Tap Save and
  check the local-save/backup guidance before changing holes.
- The two foursomes may score different holes. Check the hole selector on each
  phone. Whole-field progress advances only when all four teams complete a hole.
- In a **new .70 match**, Play's **Off low** strokes and **Net off low** scores use
  the lowest Course HCP of all four teams. Every Flamtana wager uses those net hole
  scores. Older existing matches keep full Course Net for wagers and say Legacy.
  Check the round's displayed basis before calculating manually.
- Play → **Scoreboard** shows Featured, both Groups, Foursome and Calcutta.
  Each contest compares common completed holes; Scored shows each side's own
  progress. Calcutta is a labelled projection until finalization.
- Keep the paper card current and synchronize when connection permits.

## 3. If a team says Pending

Pending means the partners' stored scores are missing or unequal. **Re-enter the
correct team gross score and Save**; a deliberate score entry resolves both
partners together. A plain Save of an untouched unequal pair preserves Pending.
If the hole was not played, deliberately clear the team score instead of inventing
one. Pending blocks final settlement. Confirm the correction reached both phones.

## 4. If the second device drops

The **host can enter or correct all four teams**, even while the foursome
assignments remain in place. Collect the second group's paper scores and enter
them on the host at the correct holes. Recover/synchronize the other phone when
possible, then check parity before finishing. Preserve its pending local writes;
do not reset its website data or reinstall it to troubleshoot a connection.

## 5. If a save says storage is full

1. Stop assuming the latest change is saved. Keep the app open and write that hole
   on paper. A failed-save message means the change is not durably stored yet.
2. Open **Library**. Export any old saved round you need to keep, then use its
   **Delete** action to remove the local copy. Prefer old, completed rounds.
   Do not delete the current event round or another unfinished round. A Shared
   Match deletion removes this phone's copy, not the shared cloud match.
3. Return to Play, re-enter the hole if necessary, and Save. Confirm save success.
   If the device remains blocked, use the host fallback and paper card.
4. For help, retry once and immediately use **More → App Support → Copy
   Diagnostics**. Include Last Local Save Failure and Last Shared Join Failure.
   Do not clear all website storage: that can destroy rounds and queued scores.

## 6. Finish, freeze and produce the Ledger Entry

1. Compare the paper card with **all four teams, all 18 holes**. Resolve every
   Pending pair. Recheck picks, stakes and tie method.
2. On both devices, Save and **Sync Now**. On the host, confirm shared score parity
   and backup status. If parity is blocked, keep the round open and use paper;
   do not force a settlement or treat a provisional payment as final.
3. On the host, choose **Complete Round / Finish Round** and confirm completion.
   The successful complete/finalize path saves the frozen RoundRecord. Confirm
   completed status and the final wager results in the Ledger Entry. Support's
   **Frozen RoundRecord** line currently checks only an active round and can say
   **No** after finishing; do not reopen or alter the round just to change that line.
4. With .72 on both phones, the remote saves the host's frozen record in Library
   and returns to **Match → Create or Join** when it receives completion. If it
   shows unconfirmed scores, captured unsaved entries or a local-save failure,
   keep it open and ask the host to check those scores. Do not clear its storage
   or force pending scores into the frozen result.
5. On the host, **Review Story** can generate an AI draft from verified team and
   wager facts when online. Use **Regenerate Story** for an earlier facts-only
   draft. Review it, then **Save Story & Preview Ledger**. Offline/service failure
   uses a facts-only draft with Memories and weather. A local-save or report-opening
   problem leaves the story screen available and explains how to retry.
   Open the **Ledger Entry Report** for the completed round. Review the four
   wagers, both Group results, card-off evidence, Calcutta and payment instructions.
   Generate/review/accept the Story of the Round if desired, then print or save PDF
   using the report's controls. Keep the PDF and paper card.
6. Check combined net positions sum to **$0**. For a new .70 round, the report's
   **Featured/Game net** uses off-low strokes; full Course Net elsewhere is
   informational. Legacy rounds use full Course Net. A card-off resolves a tied stroke-total wager; it is not
   a hole-by-hole match-play result.

## 7. Manual settlement if the app cannot settle

Keep the app's local round and paper scores. Record manual calculations and
payments separately; an unfinished app report remains provisional.

**Prepare the card.** Write each team's gross and wager net for holes 1–18, the
four net totals, stakes, tie method, saved handicap basis and eight picks.
For a new .70 match, subtract the lowest of all four saved Course HCPs from each
team's Course HCP to get its Game HCP. For example 3/6/9/12 becomes 0/3/6/9.
Use Game HCP on that team's tee stroke indexes, with no extra allowance:
`floor(Game HCP/18)` shots on every hole, plus one on indexes 1 through
`Game HCP mod 18`. Net is gross minus those shots.
For a legacy match, use its full saved HCP; a negative/plus HCP gives shots back
on the highest stroke indexes first. Use the printed/saved wager net values
whenever available. Do not convert an existing round's rules mid-event.

| Wager | Comparison | Pool and recipients |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Featured | Lowest 18-hole team net total among T1–T4 | Eight Featured stakes; winning team's two partners, or all partners of tied winning teams |
| Group 1 | T1 net total versus T2 | Four Group stakes from T1/T2; winning partners, or all four if split |
| Group 2 | T3 net total versus T4 | Four Group stakes from T3/T4; winning partners, or all four if split |
| Foursome | F1 total versus F2, calculated below | Eight Foursome stakes; winning foursome's four golfers, or all eight if split |
| Calcutta | Follows the final Featured winner(s) | Eight Calcutta stakes; divide among winning teams, then each share among that team's backers. If unbacked, pay that team's two partners |

**Foursome calculation — do this hole by hole.** F1's hole value is
`min(T1 net, T2 net)`; F2's is `min(T3 net, T4 net)`. Sum each foursome's 18
values. The lower sum wins. **Do not take the lower of two team totals.** For team
hole scores 3/6 followed by 6/3, the foursome scores 3 + 3 = 6, not 9.

**Resolve ties using the chosen rule, among the remaining tied candidates only:**

- **Split:** divide immediately.
- **Hole 18 backwards:** compare net Hole 18, then 17, continuing backwards until
  decided or all 18 comparisons exhaust.
- **Back nine:** compare net sum H10–18, then H13–18, H16–18, then H18.
- **Last six:** compare net sum H13–18, then H16–18, then H18.

For the Foursome wager, card off using the foursome's per-hole minimum series,
not either individual team's card. If the chosen method exhausts, split.
Calcutta uses the Featured result; it does not run a second card-off.

**Pay net amounts.** Divide each scoring pool among its winning golfers, then
subtract each golfer's own stake for that wager. Calcutta likewise pays gross
shares minus each person's Calcutta stake. Add all four wagers for each golfer;
each golfer participates in only their own Group comparison. Work in cents and
keep a written penny-allocation record when shares do not divide evenly; the app
uses its saved recipient-ID order. Confirm the combined total is zero before
paying. Preserve the manual worksheet for reconciliation after the event.

# v30.3.79 Constitutional Review

## Affected principles

- **Principles 1, 6, 7, 8, and 10:** the Round owns its story and immutable facts; analytics remain derived, while completed RoundRecord snapshots are not silently overwritten.
- **Principles 9 and 13:** the AI recap and saved Memories are presented as part of the Round Story with supported facts preserved.
- **Principles 11 and 12:** accepting a recap after freeze may affect local presentation, but this release does not claim to publish an authoritative amended RoundRecord. Permanent publication is deferred to an authorized Amendment Session.
- **Principles 15 and 22:** competition and settlement facts continue to come from their authoritative game records and are not inferred by the AI recap.
- **Principle 23:** no access, privacy, deletion, anonymization, or historical-preservation operation is changed.

## Compliance

The AI recap is subordinate to deterministic Round facts. It does not calculate or replace scores, handicaps, competition results, presses, SSP facts, or settlement. Accepted and draft states are visible, absence is stated honestly, and saved Memories are retained rather than silently omitted.

Round Analytics are deterministic presentation-time derivations from completed, scored holes and recorded GIR facts. Missing information is excluded, incomplete results remain provisional, and no career statistic is persisted as a duplicate fact.

For newly frozen records, recap content and status are additive optional fields. Existing frozen RoundRecords remain immutable. A later accepted recap can be displayed from the local round, but it does not mutate or masquerade as a new authoritative RoundRecord version.

No identity key, ownership role, Participant history, Device attribution, scoring assignment, Shared Match host authority, or exactly-once settlement contract changes. No historical local data is deleted, uploaded, claimed, rewritten, or deduplicated.

## Deferred constitutional work

Authoritative post-freeze recap publication requires Amendment Sessions with Owner authorization, attribution, reason, impact classification, a new RoundRecord version, and preserved prior versions. Cloud synchronization, contributor privacy controls, historical claiming, and AI event/season recaps remain deferred.

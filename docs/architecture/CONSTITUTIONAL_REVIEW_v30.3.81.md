# v30.3.81 Constitutional Review

## Scope

v30.3.81 establishes versioned competition-rule contracts, corrects WHS allowance precision for new calculations, makes Skins carryover explicit, and simplifies Match Setup readiness presentation.

## Principles affected

- **Principles 1, 6, 7, and 8:** Round facts remain owned by the Round and authoritative records remain distinct from derived calculations and explanatory UI.
- **Principle 10:** completed RoundRecords and prior game configurations are never silently overwritten. Legacy Skins configurations retain their existing behavior.
- **Principles 11 and 12:** this release does not expand the temporary reopen workflow or implement Amendment Sessions.
- **Principles 13 and 15:** each single-Round competition remains owned and finalized by its RoundRecord.
- **Principle 22:** finalized competition results and settlements are not recalculated from the new catalog. Material historical changes still require a future authorized, versioned amendment.

## Compliance conclusion

The release is additive and compatible. The rules catalog is versioned, new calculations are deterministic, and no identity, ownership, privacy, cloud, or historical-record boundary changes. No constitutional conflict was identified.

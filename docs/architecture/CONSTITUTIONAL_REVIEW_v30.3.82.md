# v30.3.82 Constitutional Review

## Scope

v30.3.82 changes presentation and navigation across Scores, Library, and More. It does not change identity, ownership, Round facts, competition facts, settlement calculations, persistence, synchronization, or cloud authorization.

## Principles affected

- **Principles 1, 6, 7, and 8:** the redesigned navigation continues to distinguish user-owned libraries from Round-owned facts. Existing RoundRecords remain authoritative.
- **Principles 2 through 5:** Account, Golfer Identity, Personal Golfer Library, roles, and participation are not merged or reinterpreted by the UI.
- **Principles 10 through 12:** completed history is not rewritten and the legacy correction workflow is not expanded.
- **Principles 13 and 15:** Scores presents the Round and its competitions without changing their ownership or finality.
- **Principle 23:** hiding or revealing a destination changes only the current view and is not deletion, withdrawal, anonymization, or archival.

## Compliance conclusion

The release is additive and presentation-only. It preserves local-first and offline-first operation, historical integrity, identity separation, and all existing access boundaries. No constitutional conflict was identified.
